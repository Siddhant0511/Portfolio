## The company

**NovaTel Communications** is a mid-sized telecom operator, the company in the assignment, holding unified access licences across five circles and offering 4G and 5G mobile, fibre broadband and enterprise connectivity to 2,800+ employees' worth of operations.

Its systems were islands: standalone billing servers, manual spreadsheets and a separate ticketing portal. The results were billing disputes, budget overruns and slow subscriber onboarding. The assignment was to design the ERP that would replace them.

<Callout>In telecom, no department creates value alone. Onboarding a subscriber touches sales, CRM, the network and finance; building a tower touches planning, procurement, QA and payables.</Callout>

## Master data: garbage in, garbage out

Every transaction references master data, so a defect there cascades everywhere downstream. I specified the key fields for each entity and what breaks if they are missing.

## CapEx governance

Telecom capital spending runs into tens of crores per circle, so the approval workflow is a rule engine, not an email chain. It enforces segregation of duties and routes requests by value.

<DataTable
  caption="CapEx approval workflow"
  head={['Step', 'Approver', 'Rule the system enforces']}
  rows={[
    ['1', 'Circle planning manager', 'Cannot submit without RF survey, lease, 3-year ROI and linked material codes'],
    ['2', 'Regional technical head', 'Endorse or reject with remarks; auto-reminder after 48 hours'],
    ['3', 'Circle finance controller', 'Hard stop: auto-reject if the request exceeds remaining circle budget by more than 15%'],
    ['4', 'VP, network operations', 'Skipped up to ₹10 lakh; mandatory above ₹10 lakh'],
    ['5', 'Chief financial officer', 'Mandatory only above ₹50 lakh'],
  ]}
/>

Every approval, rejection comment and timestamp is written to an immutable audit trail for statutory audits, TRAI filings and board oversight.
