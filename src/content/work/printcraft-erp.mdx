## The engagement

The ERP capstone cast each group as a consulting firm implementing Odoo for a client. Ours was **PrintCraft Solutions**, a commercial printing and packaging manufacturer. The brief ran end to end: understand the business, model its processes, design the solution, configure Odoo, run a full business cycle live, and recommend what comes after ERP.

## Three core processes

We modelled each process in BPMN 2.0 swimlanes, then configured Odoo so the hand-offs happen automatically.

## The live Odoo build

We configured the company, users, customers, suppliers, products, work centres and accounting in Odoo, then demonstrated four scenarios live: a custom brochure order that auto-creates a manufacturing order, routing it through press and bindery, automatic material depletion triggering an RFQ, and delivery through to a matched invoice.
