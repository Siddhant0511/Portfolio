## The brief

**StreamMax** is a fast-growing streaming platform in a crowded market. Like every subscription business, it loses users, and its churn model only notices after they have stopped watching entirely. By then, winning them back is slow and costly.

The business wanted to move from reactive to proactive retention: spot **engagement fatigue**, the gradual slide in how people use the platform, while an intervention can still work.

## Choosing the lens

We framed four possible problem statements before building anything, then picked the one the data could answer best.

Fatigued users also came back less often, with an average gap of **2.79 days** since their last session against 1.44 days, and binged less (1.11 sessions a month against 1.81). The low recommendation click rate suggested personalisation was failing precisely the users who most needed it.

## Measuring engagement in DAX

We built the model in Power BI and wrote DAX measures that turn raw behaviour into scores. The main one weights recent sessions highest, because recent usage frequency is the strongest indicator of active engagement.

```text
Engagement Score =
    0.35 * DIVIDE ( 'StreamMax'[SESSIONS LAST 7D], 21 )
  + 0.25 * DIVIDE ( 'StreamMax'[BINGE SESSIONS LAST 30D], 30 )
  + 0.25 * DIVIDE ( 'StreamMax'[UNIQUE GENRES WATCHED 30D], 15 )
  + 0.15 * ( 1 - DIVIDE ( 'StreamMax'[DAYS SINCE LAST SESSION], 30 ) )

Recency_Weighted =
    0.7 * DIVIDE ( 'StreamMax'[AVG DAILY MINUTES LAST 7D], 300 )
  + 0.3 * DIVIDE ( 'StreamMax'[AVG DAILY MINUTES LAST 30D], 300 )

Completion_Engagement =
    'StreamMax'[AVG COMPLETION RATE] * 'StreamMax'[AVG DAILY MINUTES LAST 7D]
```

Fatigue fell steadily as each score rose. Most users sat in the 0.20 to 0.30 engagement band: moderately engaged, and the most important group to watch.

## Letting the model find segments

To avoid confirming our own assumptions, we ran Power BI's **Key Influencers** visual against the fatigue label. It ranked drivers across every column and found that low recommendation click rates and falling session frequency each raise the likelihood of fatigue more than threefold. It also surfaced six high-risk segments.
